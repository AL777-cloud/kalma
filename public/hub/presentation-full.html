How It Adapts

It senses your world

  • Knows if it's morning, afternoon, or late night
  • Reads the weather and temperature where you are
  • Detects what season it is
  • Knows if you're still, walking, or active

It responds

  • Rainy evening → darker, deeper, more reverb
  • Bright morning → warmer, more open
  • Moving around → subtle energy shift
  • All changes are slow drifts, never abrupt

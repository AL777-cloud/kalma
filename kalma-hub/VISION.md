# Kalma Internal DAW & Generative Music Brain

The Kalma brain is a self-contained, local generative music engine inspired by the capabilities of modern Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs). It recreates the synthesis power of tools like Ableton Live while ensuring privacy, independence, and real-time adaptability through a BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) approach.

## Vision
- Create a lightweight internal DAW acting as the 'musical mind' of Kalma.
- Operate fully offline with optional BYOK integrations.
- Enable real-time generative and adaptive music.
- Utilize agentic AI to personalize soundscapes.
- Provide seamless integration across mobile and desktop platforms.

## Ableton Live-Style Instruments (Original Implementations)
- **Virtual Analog Synth** — Warm pads and basses.
- **FM Synthesizer** — Evolving textures and harmonic complexity.
- **Wavetable Synth** — Atmospheric and cinematic soundscapes.
- **Sampler & Granular Engine** — Nature and ambient recordings.
- **Percussion Engine** — Subtle rhythmic and pulse elements.
- **Physical Modeling Instruments** — Organic and meditative tones.

## Core Architecture of the Kalma Brain
- **Agentic AI Layer:** Interprets context and user preferences.
- **Generative Composition Engine:** Controls harmony, tempo, and arrangement.
- **Instrument & Preset Layer:** Hosts synthesizers and sound libraries.
- **Audio Engine:** Handles real-time synthesis, mixing, and spatialization.
- **Asset & Preset Management:** Local storage with tagging and versioning.

## Contextual Inputs for Adaptive Music
- **Time of Day** — Morning, afternoon, and night variations.
- **Weather & Seasons** — Integration with climate data.
- **Movement & Activity** — Adapt tempo and rhythm.
- **Holidays & Events** — Thematic sound transformations.
- **User Feedback** — Likes, dislikes, and listening behavior.

## BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) Benefits
- Enhanced privacy with on-device processing.
- Low latency for real-time audio generation.
- Reduced operational costs by avoiding recurring API fees.
- Offline functionality without internet dependency.
- Full creative control and customization.
- Optional integration with external AI services.
